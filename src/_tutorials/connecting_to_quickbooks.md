## Step-by-Step Guide: Connecting ShalomCloud to QuickBooks Online

1. **Initiate Connection to QuickBooks Online**
   - Begin by declaring your intent to connect to QuickBooks Online.
   - A switch is flipped to indicate that you're on QBO.
   [![screenshot](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/dJH24bM/medium.jpg)](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/dJH24bM/large.jpg)

2. **Log into Shalom Cloud**
   - Once the switch is flipped, log on to Shalom Cloud.
   - Navigate to the financial section, then to financial transactions.
   [![screenshot](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/yVHmgaX/medium.jpg)](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/yVHmgaX/large.jpg)

3. **Authenticate QuickBooks Connection**
   - On your first visit to financial transactions, a screen from Intuit will prompt for your QuickBooks user ID and passcode.
   - Select the company you wish to connect to.
   [![screenshot](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/WrH9R66/medium.jpg)](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/WrH9R66/large.jpg)

4. **Establish Connection**
   - If successful, a message confirms that the connection is established.
   [![screenshot](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/4wHW6vP/medium.jpg)](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/4wHW6vP/large.jpg)

5. **Configuration of Financial Categories**
   - As soon as possible, access configuration financial categories to import categories from a spreadsheet.
   [![screenshot](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/MvHJVqV/medium.jpg)](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/MvHJVqV/large.jpg)

6. **Prepare the Spreadsheet**
   - Ensure the spreadsheet is filled out before flipping the switch to minimize the gap between connection establishment and posting to QuickBooks Online.
   [![screenshot](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/gaHJGM8/medium.jpg)](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/gaHJGM8/large.jpg)

7. **Key Spreadsheet Columns**
   - Understand and fill out the important columns such as tax, donor flag, description, type, public, tran code, etc.
   [![screenshot](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/kWHwR74/medium.jpg)](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/kWHwR74/large.jpg)

8. **Specify Accounting Method**
   - For cash-based accounting, leave Debit on Charge and Credit on Charge blank.
   - For accrual-based accounting, specify accounts for debit and credit on charge.
   [![screenshot](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/B4HQlry/medium.jpg)](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/B4HQlry/large.jpg)

9. **Starting from Scratch or Conversion**
   - If starting new, fill out the spreadsheet. If converting from QuickBooks Desktop, export existing data for use.
   [![screenshot](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/JmHkQXb/medium.jpg)](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/JmHkQXb/large.jpg)

10. **Enable Document Number Feature**
    - Under Home, Declare Synagogue Options, enable the feature to auto-generate a document number.
    [![screenshot](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/7qHynLq/medium.jpg)](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/7qHynLq/large.jpg)

11. **Completion**
    - The initial setup begins with flipping the switch indicating you're on QBO.
    [![screenshot](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/zNHaJwz/medium.jpg)](https://p463.p2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/llupEPQy/artifacts/screenshots/zNHaJwz/large.jpg)
